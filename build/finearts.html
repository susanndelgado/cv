Susan Delgado

A multidisciplinary fine artist and visual storyteller whose work explores symbolism, fantasy, memory, and emotional narrative through painting, design, and immersive worldbuilding. Rooted in obsessive study, curiosity, and a devotion to craft, her work is guided by the belief that "Dreams are the Foundation of Reality."
