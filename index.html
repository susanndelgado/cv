Susan Delgado

I am a multidisciplinary fine artist, visual storyteller, designer, developer and educator whose work moves between traditional craft and digital problem-solving. My practice combines painting, illustration and narrative worldbuilding with professional experience in front-end development, responsive web design, HTML/CSS, JavaScript, PHP, UI/UX, graphic design, email development and digital production. Across both disciplines, I am driven by curiosity, continual study and the belief that strong ideas become real through craft, structure and persistence.
