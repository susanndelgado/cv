How I work

A practical UX/UI workflow grounded in implementation.

I approach interface work as a sequence of decisions: understand the content and users, establish structure, define reusable visual behavior, build responsively, then review what happens across devices and channels.

01 · DISCOVER

Context & constraints

Identify audience, business purpose, content, platform constraints, brand requirements and existing assets before designing the interface.

02 · STRUCTURE

Information & interaction

Group content into clear hierarchy, establish page or campaign architecture, and decide what users need to see, understand and do first.

03 · DESIGN + BUILD

Reusable UI patterns

Create consistent components, responsive states and visual rules, then implement them with semantic front-end code or production-ready design systems.

04 · VALIDATE

Responsive review

Check content flow, breakpoints, readability, interaction states, visual consistency and production requirements before release.
